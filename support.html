Frequently asked questions

Purchases

I bought something but did not get it

Items are delivered as soon as the App Store or Google Play confirms the payment. If it did not arrive, make sure you are online, then close the game completely and open it again: the game asks the store for unfinished purchases at start. A payment that is still "pending" at the store is delivered once the store approves it. If the item still has not arrived, email us with the order number.

How do I restore Remove Ads on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with, then tap Restore purchases in Settings or in the store. Coins, power-up packs, bundles and energy are used up when you get them, so the store cannot restore them; they come back with your progress through cloud save (see below).

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store, not by us. On iPhone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open your Google Play order history and choose the purchase. You can also email us and we will help.

Does Remove Ads remove every ad?

It removes the full-screen ads between levels for good. Reward ads, which you choose to watch for an extra energy, stay available but are never shown unless you tap them.

Progress

Will I lose my progress on a new phone or after reinstalling?

Your progress is saved on your phone and copied to your own cloud account: Google Play Games on Android (be signed in to Play Games) and iCloud on iPhone (be signed in with the same Apple ID, with iCloud turned on). After installing on the new phone, open the game while online and your levels, coins and power-ups come back. Progress does not move between Android and iPhone.

My progress did not come back

Check that you are online and signed in to the same Google Play Games profile or Apple ID you used before, then restart the game. If the cloud save was never made (for example you were not signed in on the old phone), we cannot recover it, because we do not keep a copy of your game on our side.

Playing

How does energy work?

Starting a level uses one energy; win the level and you get it back, so only a loss costs energy. You can hold up to 5. When you have fewer, one comes back every 20 minutes. When you run out you can wait, watch a reward ad for one energy, or refill with coins. The first levels do not use energy.

I am stuck on a level

Try the power-ups: ZAP clears one dot, ROW and COLUMN clear a whole line and SHUFFLE mixes the board, and none of them costs a move. Combining two specials with a swap makes a much bigger effect. When you run out of moves you can also buy five more with coins.

No sound or no vibration

Check the sound, music and vibration switches in Settings, the volume of your phone, and on iPhone the silent switch.

Privacy

What data does the game collect?

There is no account and we never ask for your name or email. Ads and anonymous gameplay statistics are provided by Google. Full details are in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstalling deletes the data on your phone. To delete your cloud save, remove the game's data in Google Play Games settings or in iCloud → Manage Storage. For anything else, email us. The privacy policy explains each step.

Privacy Policy

Dot Match by Bemty Studio · Effective 3 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Dot Match mobile game ("the game") uses, why, and who handles it. The game is made by Bemty Studio ("we", "us"). If you have any question, write to contact.bemtystudio@gmail.com.

In short
  • There is no account to create. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • Your progress is saved on your device and, if available, in your own Google Play Games or iCloud account.
  • Payments are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your card details.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses device identifiers to serve and measure ads.
  • Google Firebase collects anonymous gameplay statistics and crash reports, tied to a random id the game creates, never to who you are.
  • We do not sell your data.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device: levels completed, coins, power-ups, energy, purchases you made in the game and the player name you choose in the game, plus your settings (sound, music, vibration and display options). It also creates a random install id (a string of letters and numbers that does not contain any information about you or your device). This data stays on your device unless it is copied to your cloud save (section 2). Your player name is never sent to us.

2. Cloud save (Google Play Games and iCloud)

So you can keep your progress on a new phone or after reinstalling, the game stores a copy of your progress (everything in section 1 except your settings) in your own account:

  • on Android, in Google Play Games Saved Games, when you are signed in to Play Games;
  • on iOS, in iCloud, when iCloud is turned on for your Apple ID.

This copy is kept by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies. It is not shared with us and is not used for advertising or tracking.

3. Purchases

In-app purchases (coins, power-up packs, bundles, energy and Remove Ads) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. They handle your payment details and billing under their own policies. The game receives only a confirmation of the purchase (product and transaction reference) so it can deliver the item and avoid delivering it twice. "Restore purchases" asks the store which permanent purchases (Remove Ads) belong to your store account.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: full-screen ads between levels and optional reward ads that you choose to watch for a reward. To show and measure ads, and to prevent ad fraud, the AdMob software in the game may collect and share with Google:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID on Android;
  • your IP address and approximate (country or city level) location derived from it;
  • information about your device and operating system;
  • interactions with ads and diagnostic information.

On iOS the game does not ask for permission to track you, so it does not access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), and ads are not personalised based on your activity in other apps. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice later. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings.

Buying Remove Ads stops the full-screen ads between levels; reward ads stay available only if you choose to watch them.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Gameplay statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and to fix problems, the game uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They collect:

  • game events, for example a level started, won or lost, a power-up used, coins earned or spent, a store page opened, a purchase or an ad shown, together with the game's random install id;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, country, and app version;
  • crash logs and diagnostic information when the game stops working.

This information is not linked to your name or any account, is not used for advertising and is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites. Where consent is required, the game sends nothing to Firebase until you have given it, and nothing at all if you decline. Learn more: Privacy and security in Firebase.

6. Game settings download

The game may download a small settings file from this website (for example to adjust prices or event dates). This request sends no information about you. As with any website, the hosting provider (GitHub) receives your IP address to deliver the file; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not keep logs of these requests.

8. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

  • On your device: uninstalling the game deletes it.
  • Cloud save: on Android, open Google Play Games, go to Settings and delete the game's data (or your Play Games profile); on iOS, open Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage and delete Dot Match's data.
  • Statistics and crash reports: kept by Firebase for a limited period, then deleted automatically. You can ask us to delete them by email (see section 11).
  • Ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings at any time.

9. Children

Dot Match is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and data was collected, contact us and we will delete what we can.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. Because the game does not ask who you are, we can usually only find data linked to your install id; if you write to us, we will explain how to find it and act on your request within 30 days. We do not sell personal information. Sharing with Google for advertising is described in section 4 and can be limited with your device's ad settings.

Data handled by Google and Apple may be processed outside your country, under their safeguards for international transfers.
